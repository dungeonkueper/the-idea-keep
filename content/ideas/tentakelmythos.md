## The idea

What might a cephalopod-like civilization look like if technology developed
through cultivating habitats and ecological relationships? **Building is
gardening** frames a design direction. **Civilization is what survives you**
asks how practices, infrastructure, and responsibility could outlive short-lived
individuals. These are exploratory themes, not established alien biology or a
finished worldbuilding canon.

## Development state

The October 10 handover reports five research originals covering evolution and
environment, technology, culture and identity, adversarial xenology review, and
systems synthesis. It also reports four versions of **Die Stelle für den nächsten
Arm**, with version 4 the latest identified text. Research findings, speculative
assumptions, creative decisions, and criticism remain distinct in the background
documentation; this Egg does not reproduce or resolve them.

**The Living Reef** is the related small prototype direction: explore ecological
manipulation as a playable experiment. The migration record reports local
prototype documentation and validation, but also conflicting scopes and two
different “0.1” proposals. Those are not evidence of a unified or validated game.
The GitHub README inspected during this sweep contains only the repository title;
the handover says the local simulation still needs transfer. Code completeness
and additional cloud prototype versions remain unverified here.

## Questions kept open

- Which ecological relationships produce understandable and interesting player choices?
- What persists beyond an individual, and what mechanisms would make that plausible?
- Which prototype scope should be tested next? The existing alternatives need review.
- Which research tensions survive further scrutiny? Red-team criticism is not an automatic decision to discard a worldbuilding branch.

The migration reports missing image transfers, incomplete chat/feedback sources,
and inaccessible cloud prototype states. Those gaps remain gaps; they are not
filled with a reconstructed story or a new product decision.
